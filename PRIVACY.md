# Privacy Policy

AllergyTJ does not collect personal information.

The full privacy policy is available at **[allergy.tj/privacy](https://allergy.tj/privacy)**.

## Summary

- **No personal data collected.** No accounts, no cookies, no tracking.
- **Preferences stored on your device.** City, language, theme, and notification settings stay in your browser.
- **Location requires permission.** Coordinates are used on your device to find the nearest city.
- **Weather and air quality data** provided by [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com).
