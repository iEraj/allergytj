Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

AllergyTJ is a static website that runs entirely in your browser. There is no backend server, no database, and no user accounts. This page explains what happens with your data when you use the app.

What we collect

Nothing. AllergyTJ does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. There are no cookies, no sign-ups, no email addresses, and no usage tracking.

Local storage

The app saves a few preferences on your device using your browser's localStorage. This data stays on your device and is never sent anywhere:

  • Selected city
  • Language preference
  • Theme (light or dark)
  • Notification settings
  • Install prompt dismissal

You can clear this data at any time through your browser settings.

Weather and air quality data

When you use the app, your browser connects directly to these third-party services:

  • Open-Meteo for weather forecasts
  • Open-Meteo Air Quality API for AQI data (EU Copernicus CAMS)
  • wttr.in as a weather fallback

These requests go directly from your browser to those services. AllergyTJ never proxies, intercepts, or logs them. Each service has its own privacy policy.

GPS location

If you tap the location button, your browser will ask for permission to share your coordinates. If you grant it, the app finds the nearest city from a fixed list of 30 Tajik cities. Your coordinates are processed locally in your browser and are never sent to any AllergyTJ server. Only the nearest city's coordinates (not yours) are used to fetch weather data from Open-Meteo.

Push notifications

Notifications are generated locally in your browser based on current pollen, AQI, and UV conditions. There is no push notification server. All notification preferences are stored in localStorage on your device. Nothing fires until you explicitly opt in.

Share card

The share feature generates an image on your device using the Canvas API. If you choose to share it, that happens through your device's native share mechanism or as a file download. AllergyTJ does not see, store, or transmit the image.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel processes HTTP requests that include your IP address as part of standard internet infrastructure. AllergyTJ has no access to individual visitor data or IP addresses. Vercel Speed Insights is enabled for aggregate performance metrics (Core Web Vitals). It does not use cookies and does not collect personal information. See Vercel's privacy policy for details.

Service worker

The app installs a service worker for offline support and caching. It stores copies of the app's own files on your device so the app loads faster and works without an internet connection. The service worker does not track anything.

Children

AllergyTJ collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it here with the date. Since the app collects nothing, meaningful changes are unlikely.
