# Contributing to AllergyTJ

Thank you for your interest in AllergyTJ!

## Current Status

AllergyTJ is a solo project with a fixed development roadmap. At this time, **pull requests are not accepted** unless they address a specific open issue.

## How You Can Help

- **Report bugs** — open a [GitHub Issue](https://github.com/iEraj/allergytj/issues) with steps to reproduce
- **Suggest features** — open an issue describing the use case
- **Report inaccuracies** — if you notice incorrect pollen data, allergen information, or air quality descriptions, please open an issue
- **Translation fixes** — if you spot translation errors in English, Russian, or Tajik, open an issue with the correction

## Before Opening an Issue

1. Check [existing issues](https://github.com/iEraj/allergytj/issues) to avoid duplicates
2. Include your browser and device if reporting a UI bug
3. For data issues, include the city and what you expected vs. what you saw
